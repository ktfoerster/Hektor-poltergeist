persistent owned worker with 200,000-conflict checkpoint slices. It streams

UNKNOWN slices, while retaining an independent parent wall bound. A full
target-0 branch-0 smoke test accumulated 550,009 conflicts over eleven slices
in the same worker and stopped cleanly at its 90-second parent bound. Fifteen
